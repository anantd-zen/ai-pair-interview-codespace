# AI data notice for interview participants

The default model is OpenRouter's free endpoint: `z-ai/glm-5.2:free`.

Free endpoints are rate limited and their upstream providers may have different
data-handling policies. Do not submit confidential source code, secrets,
personal information, or other sensitive interview data.

Before each interview, confirm that the model and its current provider terms
remain appropriate. To use another model, set `PI_MODEL` in the Codespace or
change it in `.devcontainer/devcontainer.json`.
